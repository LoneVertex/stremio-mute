# Support Policy — Stremio Zero-Upload Controller

## Getting Help

1. **Check Documentation:** Read [README.md](README.md), [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md), and [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
2. **Run Diagnostics:** Execute `./scripts/diagnose.sh` to generate an issue-safe report.
3. **Open an Issue:** Submit a report via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/LoneVertex/stremio-zero-upload/issues):
   - **Bug Report:** For unexpected behavior or errors during installation/verification.
   - **Compatibility Report:** For testing results on new Stremio versions or Linux distributions.

---

## Out of Scope Support Requests

We cannot provide support for:
- Third-party Stremio addons, torrent indexers, or scrapers.
- Debrid providers or external proxy streaming configurations.
- Non-Flatpak Stremio installations (unless community contributors maintain support).
- Core Stremio application bugs unrelated to the zero-upload controller.
